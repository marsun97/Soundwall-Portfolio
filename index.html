About Me

My name is Markus Sundvall and im a music producer based in Stockholm. I found my passion for music production in my teens, where Avicii and Martin Garrix became my biggest role models. Since then, I have spent many hours in various studios, completed a Bachelor of Arts degree in music production, and traveled to London and Barcelona to work with artists and songwriters.

When it comes to recording instruments, I am independent, playing saxophone, guitar, and piano. Therefore, I have a great opportunity to help you achieve your vision, from the first demo to a finished song ready for release. I look forward to hearing more about your project!
